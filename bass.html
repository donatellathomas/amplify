About

The bass guitar emerged in the early 1950s as a modern alternative to the bulky upright bass, offering musicians greater portability and amplified sound. In 1951, Leo Fender introduced the Fender Precision Bass, the first mass-produced electric bass, which revolutionized rhythm sections by allowing bassists to be heard clearly in louder band settings. Throughout the 1960s and beyond, the instrument became a cornerstone of rock, funk, and jazz, evolving in design and technique as players pushed its role from simple accompaniment to a dynamic, expressive voice in modern music.
